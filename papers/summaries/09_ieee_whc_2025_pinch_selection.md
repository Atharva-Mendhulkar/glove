## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Finger-mounted vibration actuator (smart ring form factor) with IMU motion tracking.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Psychophysical evaluation of vibration frequency, amplitude, and timing during pinch gesture acquisition in XR.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Established perceptual guidelines showing that short, sharp transients (80–120 ms) synchronize best with user grasp intention, minimizing cognitive load.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Single-finger ring device rather than 5-finger glove array.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Provides duration and frequency guidelines for finger-specific cues, particularly setting minimum ERM burst bounds that human fingers can reliably distinguish.
