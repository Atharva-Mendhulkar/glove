## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Upper limb prosthesis equipped with force sensors and skin vibrotactile transcutaneous interfaces.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Mechanical characterization of vibration dampening in human skin tissue; human psychophysical trials measuring object stiffness discrimination.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Skin damping coefficients vary dynamically under grip force and movement, demonstrating that mechanical transmission to mechanoreceptors is non-linear.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Prosthetic socket attachment dynamics differ from flexible fabric glove coupling.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Direct mechanical explanation for why static calibration drifts during hand movement: dynamic grip force changes skin coupling and damping, necessitating motion-conditioned compensation.
