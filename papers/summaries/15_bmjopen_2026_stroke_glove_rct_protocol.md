## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Soft robotic glove with integrated vibrotactile stimulation and brain-computer interface (BCI).

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Protocol for a 48-patient, multi-center, single-blind randomized controlled trial comparing soft robotic glove with vs. without vibrotactile sensory feedback.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Defines rigorous clinical trial endpoints: Fugl-Meyer Assessment (FMA-UE), Action Research Arm Test (ARAT), and cortical functional connectivity.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Study protocol without clinical trial completion data; requires multi-center medical ethics oversight.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Serves as an essential methodological cautionary standard: establishes why an engineering validation of our glove cannot claim 'stroke rehabilitation efficacy' without a full clinical RCT.
