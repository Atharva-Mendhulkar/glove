## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Meta-analysis of 42 studies using wearable vibrotactile devices across stroke, spinal cord injury, and Parkinson's disease.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Systematic review synthesizing stimulation parameters (frequencies 20–250 Hz, burst intervals, durations) and therapeutic outcomes.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Affirms that vibrotactile stimulation promotes somatosensory cortical plasticity, but warns that cross-study comparison is severely compromised by lack of standardized reporting.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Reveals high heterogeneity in hardware configurations and lack of open reproducible benchmark datasets.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Strong justification for Research Gap G6: highlights the desperate need in the field for an open, standardized, reproducible benchmark for adaptive tactile gloves.
