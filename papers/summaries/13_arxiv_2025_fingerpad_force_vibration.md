## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Finger-mounted mechanism providing combined mechanical normal force and vibrotactile stimulation while leaving the central fingerpad bare.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Evaluated lateral skin stretch vs. dorsal vibration to preserve natural exploratory tactile perception during manipulation.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Dorsal or lateral placement maintains high discriminability while preserving fingertip tactile sensitivity for natural object manipulation.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Requires specialized multi-degree-of-freedom mechanical linkages beyond simple coin ERMs.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Validates mounting coin ERMs on the dorsal side of each finger (proximal or distal phalanx) so the palm and fingerpads remain unobstructed for grasping.
