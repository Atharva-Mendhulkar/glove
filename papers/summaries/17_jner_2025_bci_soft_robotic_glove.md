## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Soft pneumatic glove with pneumatic actuators and functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS).

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
RCT on 32 subacute stroke patients evaluating motor recovery and cortical activation changes using fNIRS before and after training.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Significant improvements in hand motor scores and increased sensorimotor cortical hemodynamic activation in the intervention group.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Pneumatic actuation requires heavy external pumps/valves; not an embedded standalone ERM glove.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Provides neurophysiological baseline data on hand motor recovery and neural activation patterns to ground our long-term research roadmap.
