# 28. Closed-loop rhythmic haptic biofeedback via smartwatch for relaxation and sleep onset

- **Authors / Year / Venue:** Biofeedback Wearable Lab (2025); *arXiv preprint / ISWC submission*

- **Record Type:** Preprint (Open Access)

## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Commercial smartwatch with linear resonant actuator (LRA) and photoplethysmography (PPG).

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Closed-loop controller adapting rhythmic tactile cadence in real time based on physiological heart rate variability (HRV).

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Closed-loop adaptation achieved significantly higher user calming efficacy than open-loop fixed rhythmic patterns.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Wrist-worn single actuator targeting autonomic relaxation rather than 5-finger spatial discrimination.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Precedent for closed-loop tactile adaptation on constrained commercial hardware without cloud inference.
