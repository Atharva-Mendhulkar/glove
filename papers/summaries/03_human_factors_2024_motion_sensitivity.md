# 03. Sensitivity to Vibrotactile Stimulation in the Hand and Wrist: Effects of Motion, Temporal Patterns, and Biological Sex

- **Authors / Year / Venue:** Tajdari, Forsyth, Lim (2024); *Human Factors (SAGE)*

## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Vibrotactile stimulators on distal fingerpads and wrist, motion tracking apparatus.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Controlled psychophysical experiment measuring vibrotactile detection thresholds and pattern discrimination under static versus dynamic motion conditions across genders.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Hand and arm motion significantly reduces vibrotactile detection accuracy and elevates perception thresholds (tactile suppression under motion).

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Conducted in controlled lab setting; tested wrist/hand locations without 5-finger independent motor multiplexing; publisher paywalled record.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Direct foundational justification for Architecture B and C: motion cannot merely be an output trigger; motion state MUST modulate stimulus intensity and timing to overcome movement-induced sensory gating.
