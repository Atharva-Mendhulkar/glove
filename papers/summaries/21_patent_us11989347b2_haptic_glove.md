## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Haptic glove featuring subdivided vibrotactile actuators arranged across individual finger segments and palm.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Multi-actuator drive circuitry coordinating spatial sensations in VR environments.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Discloses hardware layout and vibration dampening structures between adjacent finger segments.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Focuses on mechanical construction and VR scene rendering; lacks adaptive on-device human-in-the-loop ML optimization.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Key prior art for multi-channel finger actuator routing and vibration isolation.
