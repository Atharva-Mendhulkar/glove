## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Customizable multi-point haptic wearable interface.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Controlled human study testing fixed versus user-customized tactile guidance during fine motor skill acquisition tasks.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Customized tactile parameters (user-selected amplitude, pulse spacing) significantly accelerated skill acquisition and improved subjective user comfort compared to static presets.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Interactive customization required user manual adjustments rather than autonomous contextual bandit optimization.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Direct experimental evidence that per-user tactile customization is not a cosmetic feature, but measurably improves task accuracy and learning speed.
