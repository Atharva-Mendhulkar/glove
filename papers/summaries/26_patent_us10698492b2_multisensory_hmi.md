## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Wearable sensor and actuator array with calibration processor.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Per-user calibration and parameter mapping for multi-sensory wearable human-machine interfaces.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Details calibration protocols for adjusting haptic intensity according to individual user threshold curves.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Focuses on generalized calibration algorithms; does not specify embedded IMU motion-conditioned five-channel ERM scheduling.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Key prior art for user-specific haptic calibration; our system differentiates through lightweight embedded staircase calibration under strict memory bounds.
