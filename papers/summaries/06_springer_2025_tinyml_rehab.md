## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Single 6-axis IMU mounted on dorsal hand, low-power embedded microcontroller.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Extracted temporal statistical features from IMU windows and deployed a quantized TinyML model for therapeutic hand exercise recognition.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Validated that a single hand-mounted IMU without flex/contact sensors provides sufficient discriminatory information for hand movement categorization under strict resource constraints.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Abstract and proceedings record only; classification targeted therapeutic exercises rather than continuous tactile policy adaptation.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Direct hardware precedent proving that our single dorsal IMU on ESP32-S3 can accurately classify hand movement states without requiring flex sensors.
