## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Fingertip vibrating elements, at least one per finger digit.

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Control architecture delivering modular tactile feedback sequences to finger extremities.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Establishes modern glove construction techniques for integrating vibration transducers into flexible textile substrates.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Hardware integration patent; no closed-loop motion adaptation policy.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Direct technical reference for 5-channel actuator placement and textile glove wiring.
