## 1. Hardware & Sensing Setup
Survey of surface tactile display modalities (electrotactile, vibrotactile, ultrasound).

## 2. Methodology & Algorithms
Taxonomic classification of cutaneous receptor populations, perceptual masking, two-point discrimination limits, and psychophysical evaluation methods.

## 3. Reported Findings & Effect Sizes
Details two-point spatial discrimination thresholds across the hand (2–4 mm at fingertip vs. 8–10 mm at palm) and cross-modal temporal masking curves.

## 4. Limitations & Scope Caveats
Focuses primarily on electrotactile stimulation; mechanical wave propagation differs from current delivery.

## 5. Direct Relevance to ESP32-S3 Glove (Architecture C)
Provides the psychophysical basis for channel isolation: explains why 5 distinct fingers offer natural anatomical isolation compared to dense forearm arrays.
